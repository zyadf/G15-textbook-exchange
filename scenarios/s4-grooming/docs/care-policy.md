# Care and Health Policy

## We are groomers, not vets
Our groomers are trained in handling and grooming, not in veterinary medicine. If we notice skin redness, lumps, ear discharge, fleas or injuries, we will tell you what we saw, but we cannot diagnose conditions or recommend medicines. Please consult a veterinarian.

## Pets we cannot groom
We cannot groom pets that are unwell, pets that have had surgery in the last 14 days, or female dogs in heat.

## Behaviour
If a pet becomes too stressed to continue, we stop the groom and charge only for the work completed. Muzzles are used only when necessary for safety.

## Fleas and ticks
If fleas or ticks are found, the pet is bathed with a gentle shampoo and the table is disinfected. An extra cleaning fee of RM20.00 applies.
