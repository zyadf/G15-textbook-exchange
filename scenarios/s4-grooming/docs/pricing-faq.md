# Pricing FAQ

**Why do larger dogs cost more?**
Large and extra-large breeds take more time and more product, so a size surcharge is added to the base price.

**Is there a discount for new customers?**
Yes, a pet's first visit receives a discount on the base price of the service. The size surcharge is not discounted.

**Can I book back-to-back services for two pets?**
Yes. Book each pet separately; we will try to assign them to the same groomer.

**Do you accept walk-ins?**
Only for nail trims, and only when a groomer is free.

**How do I pay?**
Cash, card and DuitNow QR are accepted when you collect your pet.

**What if I am late?**
If you arrive more than 20 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment.
