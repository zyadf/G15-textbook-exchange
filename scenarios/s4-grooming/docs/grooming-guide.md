# Pawsome Grooming Studio - Guide for Owners

## Our services
We offer a bath and blow-dry, a full groom (bath, haircut, nail trim and ear cleaning), nail trims and a de-shedding treatment for double-coated breeds. Larger pets take longer, so time and price depend on the size class of the breed.

## Before your visit
Walk your dog before the appointment. Bring your pet's vaccination card: we require proof of core vaccinations given within the last 12 months. Cats must arrive in a carrier.

## During the groom
Owners wait in the lounge or collect their pet later; we message you 15 minutes before your pet is ready. Every groomed pet goes home with a bandana in the colour of the month. November is teal.

## Matted coats
Severely matted coats may need to be shaved for the pet's comfort. We will call you before shaving.

## Opening hours
Tuesday to Sunday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm. Closed on Mondays.
