# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you have vegetarian options?**
Roti canai, pisang goreng and kaya toast contain no meat. Our curry for roti canai contains fish sauce, so ask for dhal instead if you avoid fish.

**Can I reserve a table?**
We do not take reservations. Groups of more than 10 should come before 11:30 am or after 2:30 pm.

**Why is there a limit on teh tarik in the morning?**
Pulling teh tarik is done by hand by one tea master. To keep the queue moving during the morning rush on weekdays, we limit the number of teh tarik per order. Other drinks are not limited.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?**
Yes. The network is "Lakeside-Guest" and the password is printed on your receipt. Sessions are limited to 2 hours.

**Is there parking?**
Use the Block C visitor car park. The first hour is free with a stamped receipt.

**Can I order ahead?**
Yes, through this assistant. Orders placed here are prepared when you arrive at the counter and show your order ID.

**Do you sell kaya in jars?**
Yes, 300 g jars of house-made kaya are sold at the counter, subject to availability. They are not available through this assistant.
