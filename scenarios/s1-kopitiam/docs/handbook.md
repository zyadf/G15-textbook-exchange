# Kopitiam Lakeside - Customer Handbook

## Opening hours
We open daily from 6:30 am to 10:00 pm. On the first day of Hari Raya Aidilfitri and the first day of Chinese New Year we are closed. Last kitchen orders are taken at 9:30 pm; drinks are served until closing.

## Where we are
Kopitiam Lakeside is on the ground floor of Block C, next to the lake walkway. Seating is available for 64 customers indoors and 20 on the lake terrace. The terrace closes during heavy rain.

## Our coffee
Our signature kopi beans are roasted in Ipoh every Tuesday by a family roaster we have worked with since 2009, using margarine and sugar in the traditional way. Kopi is brewed in a cloth sock filter. Teh tarik is "pulled" at least six times to create the foam.

## Paying
We accept cash, DuitNow QR and Touch 'n Go eWallet. Card payments are accepted for bills of RM15.00 and above. Totals are rounded to the nearest 5 sen, following the national rounding mechanism, for all payment methods.

## Dine-in and takeaway
Dine-in customers are charged a service charge (the current rate is shown on the menu board and in the app). Takeaway drinks are packed in recyclable cups; a small container fee applies per drink. Please bring your own tumbler: we will pour your drink into it but the container fee still applies because of how our point-of-sale system is set up.

## Allergens
Our kitchen handles peanuts, shellfish, eggs, dairy, soy, fish and gluten-containing ingredients every day. We list the allergens of each item as recorded by our kitchen, but cross-contact can happen with shared woks, fryers and toasters. We cannot guarantee that any item is free from any allergen. Customers with allergies must speak to the counter staff before ordering.

## Refunds
If your order is wrong, tell the counter within 15 minutes and we will replace it. We do not give cash refunds for food that has been eaten.
