# Promotions and Policies

## Afternoon treat
Between 3 pm and 6 pm we reward larger orders with one free kaya toast. The qualifying amount is set by each outlet manager and is shown in the app. Only one free item is given per order, even for very large orders. The free toast cannot be exchanged for another item or for cash.

## Student discount
There is no student discount at this outlet. Staff meals are handled separately and are not available to customers.

## Loyalty stamps
Each paid drink earns one stamp on a paper card. Ten stamps can be exchanged for one Kopi O or Teh O. Stamps are not given for free items.

## Large orders
Takeaway orders of more than 30 items need 2 hours' notice. Please call the counter.

## Complaints
Complaints can be made to the outlet manager on duty or by e-mail to feedback@example.com. We respond within 3 working days.
