# Building Guide

## Block C (C6.xx, C7.xx)
Computer labs on level 7 have 30 to 40 workstations each. Seminar rooms on level 6 have tiered seating and lecture capture.

## Block D (D5.xx)
Small discussion rooms for 6 to 8 people, each with a whiteboard. D5.05 has a wall-mounted screen with HDMI.

## Block E (E1.11)
The large seminar room for up to 120 people, with two projectors and a wireless microphone.

## Library (L2.21)
A discussion room for 12 people on level 2 of the library. Library opening hours apply: 8:00 am to 10:00 pm on weekdays and 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on Saturdays.

## Opening hours
All teaching blocks are open from 7:30 am to 10:30 pm. Rooms can be booked from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm.
