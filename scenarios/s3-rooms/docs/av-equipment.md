# Audio-Visual Equipment

## Projectors
Rooms with projectors have HDMI and USB-C cables at the lectern. If the projector shows "No signal", press the input button on the wall panel until HDMI 1 is selected.

## Lecture capture
Seminar rooms C6.14 and C6.15 can record sessions. Recording must be requested from IT Services at least one working day before the booking.

## Faults
Report AV faults to the IT Service Desk at extension 5050. Do not unplug cables from the wall panel.

## Borrowing extra equipment
Clickers, extra microphones and webcams can be borrowed from the IT counter in Block C level 1 with your ID card.
