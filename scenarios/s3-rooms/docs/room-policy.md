# Room Booking Policy

## Who can book
Any registered student or staff member can book rooms through this assistant using their ID. Bookings are personal and cannot be transferred.

## Using the room
Arrive within 15 minutes of the start time, or the room may be released to walk-in users. Leave the room clean, switch off the projector and air-conditioning, and push chairs back under the tables.

## Cancellations
Cancel at least 2 hours before the start time if you no longer need the room. Three no-shows in one semester suspend booking rights for two weeks.

## Food and drink
Only bottled water is allowed in LAB rooms. Food is allowed in DISCUSSION and SEMINAR rooms but must be cleared away.

## Lost property
Lost property found in rooms is handed to Security at Block C, counter 2. Items are kept for 30 days.

## Accessibility
Rooms in Blocks C, D and E are wheelchair accessible by lift. L2.21 is reached by the library lift at the north entrance.
