# Condition Grading Guide

**Grade A - Like new.** No writing, no highlighting, no bent pages. Cover intact.

**Grade B - Good.** Light highlighting or pencil notes on fewer than 20 pages. Minor cover wear.

**Grade C - Fair.** Heavy highlighting or pen notes, curled pages, visible cover wear. All pages present.

**Grade D - Poor.** Missing pages, detached cover, water damage or heavy notes throughout. Sellers must describe every defect.

If in doubt between two grades, choose the lower one.
