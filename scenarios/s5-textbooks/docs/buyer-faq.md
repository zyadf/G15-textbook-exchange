# Buyer FAQ

**How do I pay?**
Pay the seller in cash or DuitNow QR at the meet-up. The platform fee is charged to your student account at the end of the month.

**Can I reserve a book?**
Expressing interest holds the listing for you for 72 hours.

**Which edition should I buy?**
Check your module outline. Older editions are cheaper but chapter numbers and exercises may differ.

**Can I return a book?**
Only if it does not match the listed condition grade, and only within 48 hours.

**Is my data shared?**
Only your student ID is visible to the seller after you express interest. E-mail addresses and phone numbers are never shown.
