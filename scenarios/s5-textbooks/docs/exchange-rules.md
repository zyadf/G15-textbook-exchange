# Campus Textbook Exchange - Rules

## How it works
Sellers list used textbooks with a condition grade and an asking price. Buyers browse listings and express interest. The platform notifies the seller, and both meet on campus to complete the exchange.

## Meeting up
Meet-ups happen only at the Library Level 1 "Book Swap Corner" between 10 am and 6 pm on weekdays. Personal contact details are never shared through the platform, and members must not ask for them.

## Pricing
The platform suggests a fair price for each listing based on the list price, the age of the book, its condition and whether a newer edition exists. Sellers may ask a little more than the suggestion, within a limit. Buyers pay a small platform fee on top of the asking price to keep the service running.

## Prohibited items
Photocopied books, solution manuals and access codes cannot be listed.

## Disputes
If the book does not match its description, the buyer can report the listing within 48 hours of the meet-up. Repeated false descriptions lead to account suspension.
