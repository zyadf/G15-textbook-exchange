# Equipment Care Guide

## Soldering stations
Set the temperature between 320 and 360 degrees Celsius for lead-free solder. Clean the tip on the brass wool, not the wet sponge, and tin the tip before switching off.

## 3D printers
Level the bed before every print. Do not leave a print running when the makerspace closes; unfinished prints are removed by the technician at closing time.

## Raspberry Pi and Arduino kits
Return all parts in the labelled box. Count the jumper wires (40 per kit). Missing parts are recorded on the loan.

## Multimeters and oscilloscopes
Switch multimeters off after use to save the battery. Never measure mains voltage in the makerspace; the bench supplies are limited to 30 V.

## Cordless drills
Charge batteries after use. Use the correct bit for the material and wear safety glasses.
