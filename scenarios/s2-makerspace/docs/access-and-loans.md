# Access and Loans

## Opening hours
The makerspace is open Monday to Friday 9:00 am to 9:00 pm and Saturday 10:00 am to 4:00 pm. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Loaned equipment can be returned through the drop box at the entrance outside opening hours; the return time is the time the technician scans it the next morning.

## Who can borrow
Any registered member can borrow equipment. Your member ID (format MKR-nnnn) is printed on your access card.

## Loan periods and fees
Loan periods depend on the type of equipment and on member rules set for this semester; the assistant tells you the exact due time when you borrow. 3D printers are loaned for short print jobs only. Late returns are charged per started hour, with a cap per loan. Unpaid fees block new loans.

## Damaged equipment
Report damage when you return the item. Accidental damage is not charged the first time. Repeated damage may lead to suspension of loan privileges.

## Consumables
PLA filament is provided free up to 250 g per member per week. Solder and flux are provided at the benches. Laser cutter materials must be bought from the counter (3 mm plywood or acrylic only).
