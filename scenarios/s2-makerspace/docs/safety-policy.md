# Makerspace Safety Policy

## Safety quiz
Before using soldering stations, the hot-air rework station or the laser cutter, every member must pass the online safety quiz with a score of at least 80%. The quiz takes about 20 minutes and can be retaken after 24 hours. Your quiz status is recorded against your member ID.

## Personal protective equipment
Safety glasses are mandatory at soldering benches and near the laser cutter. Closed-toe shoes are required in the workshop at all times. Long hair must be tied back when using drills or printers.

## Fire safety
Never leave a soldering iron or the laser cutter unattended while it is on. A CO2 extinguisher is mounted beside the laser cutter and a fire blanket beside each soldering bench. The makerspace fire assembly point is the Lakeside Cafe car park, Gate C.

## Fumes
Use the fume extractor whenever you solder. Lead-free solder is the only solder allowed.

## Incidents
Report every injury, burn or near miss to the technician on duty, even if it seems minor. Incidents are logged for safety review only.
